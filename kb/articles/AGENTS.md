# Contents

- Source captures live in one descriptive directory per capture, with Markdown, provenance, and any local assets kept together.

# Guidelines

- Keep each capture self-contained and record its original source, capture date, completeness, and warnings.
- Keep local assets beside their capture and use relative links from the capture Markdown.
- Do not commit cookies, credentials, browser profiles, raw authenticated DOM, HAR files, or other session state.
- Preserve captured wording and source authority. Put personal analysis in a separate linked note or a clearly labeled annotation.
- Never present a partial capture as complete.
